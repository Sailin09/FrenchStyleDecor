# Curtain Seven-Image Template

Use this template for FrenchStyleDecor curtain listings. Generate exactly seven separate square images, never a collage.

## Product invariants
- Use the supplied product photo as the source of truth for color, motif scale, construction, header style, hem, transparency, and panel proportions.
- Preserve the original design. Do not enlarge, redraw, simplify, recolor, or distort the pattern.
- Keep the curtain single-layer unless the source product clearly includes a second layer. Do not add a rear curtain or lining.
- Use realistic, everyday viewing angles and French-style interiors. Every image must have a clearly different composition and camera angle.
- No text, logos, watermarks, labels, or stitched-together layouts.
- For flocked patterns, show a soft raised velvet-like nap, subtle tactile relief, and matte light-catching contrast while preserving the repeat and scale.

## Seven images
1. **Front overall** — straight-on full window or door, complete curtain length and header visible.
2. **Room angle** — three-quarter view from the opposite side, showing depth and natural folds.
3. **Lifestyle angle** — a distinct lived-in French room composition with furniture, clearly different camera height and framing.
4. **Side/drape view** — one panel naturally opened or tied, emphasizing fall, hem, and translucency.
5. **Header/detail view** — medium framing of the actual top construction (rod pocket, ruffles, tabs, or other supplied construction), with accurate proportions.
6. **Lower/hem detail** — medium framing showing the lower edge, fullness, folds, and finished hem in context.
7. **Fabric detail** — medium-distance textile sample presentation, showing several complete repeat motifs, weave, and edge/fold. Do not use an extreme macro crop or a generic room close-up. For flocked fabric, make the raised nap visibly tactile but subtle.

## Quality check
Compare every image with the source product: motif scale, color, transparency, header construction, hem, and panel count must match. Reject duplicate angles, altered construction, oversized motifs, added layers, and implausible geometry.
